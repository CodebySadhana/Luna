# Luna Hook

Blair's skill. The single highest-leverage object in content: one sentence
that decides whether anything else you made gets seen.

## The rule

**A hook is not a summary. It is a debt.**

It promises something the rest of the piece has to pay off. A line that
describes the content is a title. A line that opens a gap is a hook.

If the piece cannot pay the debt, the hook is clickbait — it converts once and
costs trust permanently.

## The mechanisms

Every working hook runs at least one. Name which one you used.

| Mechanism | How it works | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Contradiction** | Violates a belief they hold | "The productivity advice that makes smart students *less* productive" |
| **Open loop** | Names an answer, withholds it | "I found out why nobody replied. It wasn't the message." |
| **Recognition** | Names a private experience | "You reread your own message after sending it." |
| **Specific number** | Precision implies evidence | "43 of 50 had never opened settings." |
| **Stakes** | Something is at risk, now | "This costs you a client and you never hear about it." |
| **Confession** | Admits a real cost | "I spent eight months on the wrong audience." |
| **Named enemy** | Gives the frustration a target | "It's not your discipline. It's the default settings." |

Two mechanisms in one line is usually stronger than one. Three is a mess.

## The upgrade passes

Run a weak hook through these in order. Most hooks fail at the first.

1. **Cut the first three words.** "In this video I'll show you how to…" → the
   line starts at word seven. Almost always an improvement.
2. **Concrete the abstract.** "a lot of money" → "$4,200". "students" → "the
   ones who take the best notes". "recently" → "on a Tuesday in March".
3. **Find the belief to violate.** What does the audience assume that this
   contradicts? That's the line.
4. **Singular you.** Never "you guys," "everyone," or "people."
5. **Read it aloud, mid-scroll, no context.** If it needs setup, it isn't a
   hook.
6. **Ask what debt it creates** — and whether the piece pays it.

## The test

> Would a stranger who does not know you, does not care about this topic, and
> was about to scroll — stop?

"It's clear and accurate" is not a pass. Clear and accurate is the floor.

## Output

```
CURRENT   "[their line]"
DIAGNOSIS [what specifically fails — one line, no cushioning]

01  "[hook]"                          [mechanism]
02  "[hook]"                          [mechanism]
03  "[hook]"                          [mechanism]
04  "[hook]"                          [mechanism]
05  "[hook]"                          [mechanism]

PICK  02 — [why this one, in terms of what the viewer does]
DEBT  [what the piece must now deliver to pay this off]
```

Five to eight variants. Ranked. Never an unranked list — picking is the job.

## Boundaries

Hooks only. Not the full script (`/luna-script`), not the concept
(`/luna-ideas`). If the underlying idea is dead, say so and stop — a great
hook on a dead idea is a wasted great hook.

Reads `luna/memory/voice.md` and `audience.md` when they exist. The audience's
own verbatim phrasing beats anything you invent.
