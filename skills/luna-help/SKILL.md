# Luna Help

Display this card when invoked. One-shot — do NOT change mode or persist
anything.

## Levels

| Level | Trigger | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Soft** | `/luna soft` | Does what's asked, names the sharper alternative. You pick. |
| **Full** | `/luna` | The ladder enforced. Verdicts. Weak ideas killed. Default. |
| **Ruthless** | `/luna ruthless` | Kills first, builds second. Challenges the brief itself. |

Level sticks until changed or session end.

## The ladder

```
1  Does anyone actually care?          7  Does it strengthen the brand?
2  Who specifically cares?             8  Can the idea be sharper?
3  Why would they stop?                9  Can execution be more distinctive?
4  Why would they keep watching?      10  TEST
5  Why would they share it?           11  LEARN
6  Why would they remember it?        12  REPEAT
```

> Don't create content. Create reasons for people to care.

## Deactivate

Say "stop luna" or "normal mode". Resume with `/luna`. `/luna off` works too.
