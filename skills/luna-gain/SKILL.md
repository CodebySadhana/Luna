# Luna Gain

`/luna-debt` finds what is quietly costing them. This finds what is quietly
available.

**Ranked by leverage: return divided by effort.** Not by size of prize — a
solo operator cannot spend a big prize they have no hours for.

## Where gains hide

**1 — The silent strength.** The one distinctive thing they did once and never
repeated. Almost every account has one. It is usually the highest-return
finding in this whole skill, and it is usually invisible to the person who
made it.

**2 — Format gaps.** A format that suits their material and that nobody in
their niche is using. Carousels in a video-only niche. A teardown series where
everyone does listicles.

**3 — Audience signals being ignored.** The question that keeps appearing in
comments. The post that overperformed and was never followed up. **The most
reliable gain in the list, because the audience already told them.**

**4 — Distribution left on the table.** Work already made that never reached a
second surface. Zero new production cost.

**5 — Content gaps.** What the audience is asking about that nobody in the
niche answers well — usually because it is unglamorous.

**6 — Growth loops.** A mechanism where output creates input: content from
comments, a series people wait for, something worth being sent.

**7 — The unconverted.** An audience that watches and is never given a next
step. Common and quietly expensive.

## Rank by leverage, not size

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `free` | Costs nothing. Do it today. |
| `cheap` | Hours, not days. |
| `real` | Genuine work, genuinely worth it. |
| `later` | Real gain, wrong sequence. Name what comes first. |

Lead with `free`. A solo operator who is given a list starting with a
three-week project does none of it.

## Output

```
01  free   Post 8 (the teardown) is the most distinctive thing on this
           account and there is exactly one. Make it a recurring format.
           This is the finding.
02  free   Same question in 6 comment threads, never answered in a post.
           They told you what to make. Make it.
03  cheap  Nothing from the last 3 months exists on a second surface.
           Same work, second audience.
04  cheap  No next step anywhere. 4,000 people watch and are asked for
           nothing. One line in the bio changes this.
05  real   Niche is entirely video. Carousels are open and your material
           suits them.
06  later  Email list. Highest long-term value here, but positioning
           comes first — see /luna-strategy.

BIGGEST   [the one to do this week]
```

## Honesty

**Never invent an opportunity to fill the list.** Three real gains beat six
padded ones. If they are already doing the obvious things well, say so — "you
are doing this right, the gain here is patience" is a legitimate output.

No fabricated numbers, no projected results, no "this could 10x your reach."

## Boundaries

Reports, changes nothing. One-shot. Pairs with `/luna-debt` — run both for a
full picture of what to stop and what to start.
