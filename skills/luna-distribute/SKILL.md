# Luna Distribute

Olivia's skill.

**Publishing is not distribution.** Posting puts a piece where your existing
audience might see it. Distribution is everything you do to get it past that
boundary. Most operators do the first and call it the second.

## Designed in, not bolted on

The best time to run this is **before the piece is made**. A piece built to be
quotable travels. A piece built to be watched does not.

If the piece already exists, work with what's there — but say what would have
been designed differently.

## The travel mechanism

Every distributed piece needs one. Name it explicitly:

| Mechanism | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Sendable** | One specific person needs to see this. Names a situation precisely enough that someone thinks of a friend. |
| **Quotable** | One line that survives being extracted and screenshotted. |
| **Arguable** | A position people want to respond to. Highest reach, highest risk. |
| **Referenceable** | Worth saving and coming back to. Slow, durable. |
| **Timely** | Attached to something happening now. Fast, expires. |

**No mechanism = no distribution.** Posting harder does not substitute.

## The channel ladder for a small account

| Channel | Good for | Real cost |
|---|---|---|
| Your own feed | Depth with people who already chose you | Low |
| A second platform, adapted | Doubles reach on work already made | Low |
| Replies on larger accounts | Free, repeatable, badly underrated | Low, needs substance |
| Communities (Reddit, Discord, Slack) | High-intent niche reach | Needs genuine participation first |
| Collaboration | Borrowed trust, fastest new reach | Needs a real relationship |
| Email | The only audience a platform can't take away | Low |
| Paid | Predictable — and amplifies whatever it's given | Money |

**Email is the asset. Everything else is rented.** Raise this whenever an
operator's entire audience lives on one platform.

## Adaptation, not cross-posting

Same insight, native form. A cross-post with the watermark still on it reads
as "I made this for somewhere else."

For each secondary surface, say what actually changes: the length, the
opening, the format, the ending.

## Outreach

Small operators get ignored because their outreach is about themselves.

**Lead with something already made.** A specific reference to their work, an
insight they can use, or a piece you already published about them. Never
"would love to collaborate."

Match by audience overlap and stage, not follower count. A peer is a
collaborator. Someone ten times larger is a lottery ticket.

## Output

```
MECHANISM   [why this travels — from the table, or none, said plainly]

PRIMARY     [channel] — [format, timing]
SECONDARY   [channel] — [what changes: length, opening, ending]
THIRD       [channel] — [what changes]

OUTREACH    [one person or community]
            Opening line: "[the actual line, written]"

BUILT IN    [what in the piece makes someone send it to one person]
IF FLAT     [what to try before concluding the idea was wrong]
```

## Boundaries

No spam, no pods, no engagement rings, no DM blasts, no posting the same thing
to five subreddits. These work briefly and cost the audience you wanted.
