# Luna Viral

## Read this first

**Nobody can make something go viral.** Not Luna, not an agency, not the
person whose last one did. Spread is a high-variance outcome of a mechanism
plus timing plus luck, and the luck term is large.

What this skill does: design for **asymmetric upside** — a concept whose
downside is one ordinary post and whose upside is uncapped. Then say honestly
what it costs if it doesn't land.

If you want a guaranteed number, no honest tool can give you one.

## The mechanism is the whole job

Content does not spread because it is good. It spreads because **sharing it
does something for the sharer.**

Name the sentence the sharer is implicitly saying. If you can't, there is no
mechanism, and the piece will not travel no matter how well it is made.

| Mechanism | What sharing says |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | "This is me." / "This is us." |
| **Status** | "I knew this first." / "I'm not fooled by this." |
| **Utility** | "You specifically need this." |
| **Argument** | "This is wrong and here's why." |
| **Recognition** | "How does someone know this about me?" |
| **Awe** | "You have to see this." |
| **Usefulness to a group** | "This is exactly what we were arguing about." |

**Identity and argument have the highest ceilings.** Argument also has the
highest cost — it brings an audience that came for the fight.

## Asymmetry, honestly

A good big swing:

- **Costs no more than a normal piece.** If the swing takes three weeks, it
  isn't asymmetric, it's a gamble with your month.
- **Is publishable even if it does nothing.** It should still be a good post
  at 800 views (illustrative — Luna never predicts a number).
- **Doesn't burn anything if it lands wrong.** No manufactured outrage, no
  positions you'd have to walk back, nothing that brings the wrong audience
  permanently.

**The last one disqualifies most "viral" advice.** Reach bought with a take
you don't hold costs more than it returns, and the bill arrives later.

## The honest downside

Every concept from this skill states what it risks:

- What audience does this attract, and do you want them?
- If this reaches 50x normal, are you glad it has your name on it?
- Would you defend this in six months?

## Output

```
CONCEPT     [the idea]
MECHANISM   [from the table — and the sharer's implicit sentence, written]
UPSIDE      [what happens if it lands — described, never numbered]
FLOOR       [what it is if it doesn't. Should still be a good post.]
COST        [production effort vs a normal piece]
RISK        [what it attracts, what it might cost]
TIMING      [why now, or: no timing advantage — inferred]

VERDICT     WORTH THE SWING / NOT ASYMMETRIC / TOO EXPENSIVE
```

## Never

Promise reach. Estimate views. Say "this will go viral." Recommend
manufactured controversy, engagement bait, stolen content, or a take the
operator doesn't hold.

The honest sentence, when asked: **"I can tell you this one has a mechanism.
I can't tell you it will work."**

## Boundaries

One concept, not ten. A big swing is a decision, not a menu. One-shot.
