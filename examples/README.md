## The pattern in all of them

Luna's leverage is almost never the words. It is the refusal to start writing
before she knows who the piece is for and why they would stop.

Every example here has the same shape:

```
the brief          what was asked for
the refusal        which rung it failed
the observation    what the audience actually said
the version        what to make instead
the mechanism      why that one works
```
