# One Idea, Many Pieces

Repurposing without cross-posting.

*Illustrative example. No performance data is claimed.*

---

## The brief

> "I made a 12-minute YouTube video about why most home gym setups waste money.
> How do I get more out of it?"

---

## `/luna-repurpose`

```
INSIGHT   People buy equipment for the training they imagine doing,
          not the training they actually do. The waste isn't in the
          price, it's in the mismatch.

That's the thing that transfers. Everything below is a different
container for it.
```

### FLAGSHIP — YouTube, 12 min (exists)

```
What only fits here: the full cost breakdown, the three real
setups, the arithmetic. Depth is the reason this version exists.
```

### NATIVE — Short vertical video, 35s

```
Hook (rewritten — the long-form opener will not survive here):
  "You bought that for the person you were going to become."

What changes:  one setup instead of three. No arithmetic. The
               turn lands at 9 seconds instead of 90.
Ending:        lands on the line. No "full video on my channel"
               as the whole payoff — say what's in it or say
               nothing.
```

### NATIVE — LinkedIn text, 160 words

```
Hook (rewritten again — third different opener):
  "I spent $2,400 on equipment I used eleven times.

  Here's the mistake, and it isn't discipline."

What changes:  it becomes a decision-making post, not a fitness
               post. The audience here is interested in the
               reasoning error, not the squat rack.
```

### EXTRACT — Carousel, 6 slides

```
Slide 1:  "You bought that for the person you were going to become"
Slides 2-5: one piece of equipment each, what it costs, what it
            actually gets used for
Slide 6:  the one thing worth buying first

Stands alone. Never seen the video? Still complete.
```

### ATOMS — nearly free, most skipped

```
- Reply on any "what should I buy for a home gym" post:
  "Whatever you'll use on a bad day. Buy for your worst week,
   not your best one."

- Standalone text post:
  "Home gym rule: buy for your worst week."

- Comment reply that becomes its own post if it lands.
```

### DON'T

```
Pinterest. This is an argument, not a plan or a product. Pinterest
rewards searchable, actionable, visual — an opinion piece has no
job to do there.

Also don't make a second short from the same video this month.
The insight is one idea. Two shorts from it is the same post
twice, and your audience notices before your analytics do.
```

---

## What to notice

**The hook is rewritten three times.** Not adapted — rewritten. Each platform
has a different posture, and a line tuned for one fails completely on another.
This is the single most-skipped step in repurposing.

**The tiers are not equal.** One flagship, two real adaptations, one extract,
three near-free atoms. Effort descends. Most people try to make ten flagships
and produce two.

**The atoms are flagged as the most-skipped tier.** A reply costs thirty
seconds and is where small accounts actually get found.

**DON'T is included.** Knowing where an idea doesn't belong prevents the
scattergun instinct that makes an operator look inconsistent everywhere.

**The self-cannibalization warning.** "Two shorts from one insight is the same
post twice" — the honest limit on repurposing, which most advice on the
subject never mentions because it's selling volume.
