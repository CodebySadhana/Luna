# The Luna Ladder

Ponytail's ladder asks: *can this be built with less code?*

Luna's ladder asks: **does this deserve anyone's attention?**

Run it top to bottom before anything gets made. **Fail a rung, stop there.**
The rung you fail is the finding — don't quietly fix it in your head and keep
climbing. Report which rung failed and why.

---

### 1 — Does anyone actually care?

Not "is this on brand." Not "is this true." Does a real person have a real
reason to stop what they were doing?

If the honest answer is "it's fine, it's useful," that's a no. Useful is the
floor, not the reason.

**Fail → KILL.** No amount of execution rescues an idea nobody wanted.

### 2 — Who specifically cares?

Name them. Not a demographic — a situation.

- ❌ "Founders aged 25–40"
- ✅ "Founders three months into building in public who have stopped getting comments and don't know why"

If you can't name the situation, you don't have an audience, you have a guess.

**Fail → back to OBSERVE.** Research, don't ideate.

### 3 — Why would they stop?

The first two seconds. What is the specific tension, contradiction, promise,
or recognition that interrupts a scroll?

"Interesting topic" is not a reason to stop. A reason to stop is structural:
something is wrong, something is backwards, something names an experience they
thought was theirs alone.

**Fail → the hook is dead, not the idea.** Route to `/luna-hook`.

### 4 — Why would they keep watching?

Attention is granted in installments. What is the second reason, at 5 seconds?
The third, at 15?

If the whole idea is spent in the hook, you have a caption, not a piece.

**Fail → the idea is thinner than the hook. Deepen or cut to a shorter format.**

### 5 — Why would they share it?

People don't share content. They share **self-expression**. A share says
something about the sharer: I'm smart, I'm early, I'm not fooled, this is me,
this is us.

Name the sentence the sharer is implicitly saying.

**Fail → it can still be good. Not everything must travel. But know that now,
and don't budget for reach you have no mechanism to earn.**

### 6 — Why would they remember it?

A week later, what survives? A phrase, a frame, a number, an image, a name?

Forgettable content that performs is still a loss — it bought attention and
banked nothing.

**Fail → find the one thing worth remembering, and build around it.**

### 7 — Does it strengthen the brand?

Would someone who saw ten of these know what you stand for?

Virality without brand association is a donation to the platform.

**Fail → it's someone else's content wearing your logo. Rework or kill.**

### 8 — Can the idea be sharper?

Not longer. Sharper. Narrower audience, bolder claim, more specific detail,
more uncomfortable truth.

The first version of an idea is almost never the sharp one.

**Always run this rung. It is the highest-return step in the ladder.**

### 9 — Can the execution be more distinctive?

Same idea, a format nobody in this niche is using. Same script, a structure
that doesn't sound like everyone else's.

**Fail → you'll compete on the algorithm's terms instead of your own.**

### 10 — TEST

Publish it as an experiment with a written expectation. What do you expect,
and what result would change your mind?

An expectation written *after* the result is not a test, it's a story.

### 11 — LEARN

Compare result to expectation. Record the delta, not the number.

WIN / LOSS / PATTERN / HYPOTHESIS → memory.

### 12 — REPEAT

Feed the learning back into rung 1. The ladder gets smarter every cycle,
because rungs 1–3 are answered by memory instead of guessing.

---

## The core rule

> **Don't create content. Create reasons for people to care.**

## Verdicts

The ladder produces exactly one of three outcomes:

| Verdict | When |
|---------|------|
| **MAKE IT** | Rungs 1–7 clear. 8 and 9 applied. |
| **REWORK IT** | The insight is alive but a rung failed for a fixable reason. Name the rung. |
| **KILL IT** | Rung 1 or 2 failed, or three-plus rungs failed. Say why in one line and move on. |

Never return "here are some thoughts." Return a verdict.
