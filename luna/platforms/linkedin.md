# LinkedIn

## Posture
At work, or thinking about work. Reading between other things. Professionally
motivated — status, career, competence, and the quiet question "am I doing
this right?"

The audience is more willing to read actual text here than anywhere else.

## Constraints that shape content
- Text posts truncate after roughly two or three lines behind "see more." The
  break point is the entire hook.
- Documents (PDF carousels) get real attention and are under-used.
- Links in the body are widely believed to suppress reach — treat that as an
  observation, not a law, and put the link in the first comment if you care.
- Comments from your own network surface a post to their networks.

## What the format rewards
**Text that carries a specific, credible, first-hand claim.** Not thought
leadership — a thing you actually did, with the number attached.

The highest-performing shape on this platform is: *I did a specific thing,
here is what happened, here is what I got wrong.*

## Hooks
Everything before "see more." Two lines. Make them a complete tension.

```
BAD   "I've been thinking a lot lately about the importance of company
      culture and how it affects team performance..."

GOOD  "We lost our best engineer in month four.
      The exit interview named a thing I'd been told twice and ignored."
```

## Structure that works
```
2 lines    the tension — before the fold
1 line     white space
body       short paragraphs, one idea each, no walls
the turn   what you got wrong, or what surprised you
close      the reusable principle, stated plainly
```

Line breaks are formatting on this platform. Dense paragraphs do not get read.

## For a solo operator
The best platform in social for turning a small audience into actual business,
because the reader is already in a professional frame. A B2B founder with
2,000 relevant followers here has more commercial reach than 50,000 on a
lean-back platform.

## Don't
- Fake vulnerability. It is the most parodied thing here, for a reason.
- "Agree?" at the end
- The one-line-per-paragraph gimmick on a post with no substance
- The manufactured emotional build-up. This audience has antibodies now.
