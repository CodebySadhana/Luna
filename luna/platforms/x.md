# X

## Posture
Fast, argumentative, and industry-adjacent. People are here for takes,
information, and conflict. Attention spans are short, but the audience is
often the most professionally relevant one you can reach.

## Constraints that shape content
- Short by default; the first post of a thread carries everything.
- Quote-posts amplify by disagreement as easily as by agreement.
- Replies are the primary distribution mechanism for small accounts.
- Content half-life measured in hours. Almost nothing lives past a day.

## What the format rewards
**A position, stated cleanly.** This is the platform most suited to Principle
12 — say the uncomfortable thing. Agreement travels here far less than
disagreement does.

**Threads** work when each post stands alone. A thread that requires reading
in order loses everyone by post three.

## Hooks
The first post is the entire pitch. It should be complete on its own — if it
only works as a setup for post two, it fails.

## For a small account
**Replies are the growth mechanism, not posts.** A substantive reply to a
large account is seen by that account's audience. This is the most reliable
path from zero on this platform, and it costs nothing but judgment.

The reply has to add something. "Great thread!" is noise. A specific
counter-example, a correction, or an extension is a free audition.

## Structure that works
- **Single post:** one claim, no preamble, no thread hook. The best posts here
  are one sentence.
- **Thread:** each post a complete unit, ordered by escalating specificity,
  ending on the strongest — not on a CTA.

## For a solo operator
Best used as a thinking-in-public surface: where ideas get tested cheaply
before becoming real content elsewhere. A post that gets traction here is a
validated idea for a video or a newsletter.

## Don't
- Manufacture conflict for reach. It works, and it costs you the audience you wanted.
- Engagement bait ("RT if you agree")
- Thread hooks that promise more than the thread contains
- Post the same take as everyone else, 40 minutes later
