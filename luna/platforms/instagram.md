# Instagram

## Posture
Lean-back and social. People open it to see what's happening, not to solve a
problem. Intent is low, tolerance for polish is high, and the audience is
often people who already know you.

## Constraints that shape content
- Vertical 9:16 for Reels; 4:5 is the largest feed footprint for stills.
- No clickable links in captions. Link in bio, or stickers in Stories.
- Carousels get a second impression: a swipe re-surfaces the post.
- Muted autoplay is the default. Captions are not optional.
- Text on the lower third gets covered by the UI.

## What the format rewards
**Saves and sends**, more than likes. A save is reference value; a send is
someone spending social capital on you. Both are structural: build content
that is worth returning to or worth showing one specific friend.

**Carousels** are the most underrated format for a small account — slide one
is a hook, and the swipe is a low-cost commitment that the interface makes
almost free.

## Hooks
First frame must be legible at thumbnail size and readable in half a second.
Text on the frame does more work than the spoken first line, because sound
starts off.

## Structure that works
- **Reel:** hook frame → concrete detail by 3s → turn by 10s → land it. Under
  30s unless the idea genuinely needs more.
- **Carousel:** slide 1 hook, slide 2 the stakes, slides 3–7 one idea each,
  final slide the thing worth remembering. Not a CTA slide — a payoff slide.
- **Stories:** where the relationship gets built. Lowest production cost,
  highest trust return. Use it for the messy version.

## For a solo operator
Instagram is where an audience gets *deeper*, not wider. Reach here is harder
to earn cold than on TikTok. Use it for identity, recurring format, and the
community you already have.

## Distinctive signal
A recognizable visual system matters more here than anywhere else. Same type,
same color, same frame. People should know it's yours mid-scroll.

## Don't
- Cross-post a TikTok with the watermark
- Engagement-bait captions ("comment YES")
- Bury the point below three lines of setup — the caption truncates
